02

Website purpose

This website presents FBridge's raw ingredient supplier sourcing service for European food and beverage companies. It also provides an ingredient request form, an email address for questions and a route to arrange a call through an external scheduling provider.

Website content is general information. A sourcing engagement, including its scope, supplier requirements, success fee and responsibilities, only begins under the commercial terms agreed with the client.
