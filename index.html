About us

How FBridge started.

FBridge grew out of weBridge, a leading go-to-market consultancy working with food and beverage companies on client acquisition.

While helping clients grow, we kept seeing the same problem: finding the right ingredient suppliers. We put our business development and sales experience to work on it, connecting clients with producers worldwide, especially across Latin America and Africa.
