For buyers

Better sourcing starts with a sharper brief.

A long supplier list is not much use if nobody knows whether the factory can make the product, export it, or answer buyer questions. We narrow the request first, then make the first calls count.

The brief

What needs to be made?

Spec, volume, certifications, packaging, target price, timeline, and the person making the decision.

The shortlist

Who is worth a call?

Production fit, export basics, responsiveness, documentation, and whether the factory can answer buyer questions.

For manufacturers

Make the factory easier to understand from abroad.

A capable producer can still be hard to evaluate from another country. FBridge turns production strengths into clear buyer material, then runs focused outreach around the categories that fit.

Profile

Make the offer clear

Category, capacity, export history, certifications, MOQ, sample process, and pricing context.

Pipeline

Open the right doors

Target lists, first messages, reply handling, and follow-up for the buyers that match the factory.

Follow-up

Keep interest moving

Sample requests, calls, quote comparison, and the next action kept visible for both sides.

Priority regions

Start with the lanes where there is real product depth.

FBridge is not trying to cover every food category in every country. The first lanes are narrow: products with enough manufacturer depth, buyer demand, and export logic to make a sourcing sprint worthwhile.

Latin America

Coffee, cacao, fruit, drinks, sauces, and private-label production.

Starting markets include Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Brazil, Chile, and nearby export hubs.

Africa

Cocoa, coffee, specialty ingredients, packaged foods, and export-ready factories.

Starting markets include Morocco, Egypt, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, and regional production clusters.

Process

A narrow sourcing sprint, run by people.

01

Write the brief.

We clarify the product, volumes, must-haves, nice-to-haves, and timeline.

02

Find and check suppliers.

We look for production fit, export readiness, responsiveness, and the basic documents buyers expect.

03

Move samples and quotes.

We keep communication moving so the buyer can compare real options instead of chasing loose replies.

04

Prepare the handoff.

When there is a fit, we help both sides move into negotiation, documentation, or ongoing support.

Start small
