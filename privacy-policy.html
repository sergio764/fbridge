Privacy policy

How we use personal data when you visit the website, send an enquiry or arrange a call.

01

Data controller

The controller is WEBRIDGE SOCIEDAD LIMITADA., NIF B26614370 and VAT ESB26614370, with registered office at The Terminal Hub, C. de la Travesía, 46024 Valencia, Spain. You can contact us about privacy at fbridge@webridge.es.

02

Data we process

Depending on how you interact with FBridge, we may process:

  • Identity, role, company name and business contact details that you provide.
  • The contents of your enquiry, including ingredient requirements, specifications, volumes and timing.
  • Appointment information that you submit directly through Cal.com.
  • Communications, supplier notes and commercial records created while assessing or delivering a sourcing engagement.
  • Basic technical and security logs processed by the website host when pages are requested.

The ingredient request form sends the details you choose to provide to a weBridge-controlled endpoint hosted by Vercel. The endpoint validates the request and uses Resend to deliver it to FBridge by email. The website does not run advertising or analytics scripts. Clicking the booking link takes you to Cal.com.

03

Purposes and legal bases

Reply to enquiries and assess a possible supplier search Steps requested before a contract and our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries.
Provide and manage an agreed sourcing service Performance of a contract.
Maintain accounting, tax and legal records Compliance with legal obligations.
Protect the website and our business operations Our legitimate interest in security, fraud prevention and service continuity.

04

Service providers and international transfers

Personal data may be processed by providers that support our operations, such as email, scheduling, website hosting, customer relationship management, accounting, legal and IT services. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. Ingredient requests pass through Vercel's hosting infrastructure and are delivered by Resend, operated by Plus Five Five, Inc. Appointment scheduling is provided by Cal.com when you choose that link. You can review Vercel's privacy notice and Resend's privacy policy.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on an adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism under the GDPR. We do not sell personal data.

05

How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Enquiries are retained while they remain relevant; client and commercial records are kept for the duration of the relationship and any legally required limitation, accounting or tax period. Data may be retained longer where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Website, email-delivery and appointment providers may retain technical, security or delivery records under their own retention policies and applicable agreements.

06

Your GDPR rights

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send a request to fbridge@webridge.es. We may need to verify your identity before completing it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

07

Cookies, external links and automated decisions

FBridge does not currently place optional analytics or advertising cookies on this website. The hosting provider may process essential technical information needed to deliver and secure the pages. Submitting the ingredient request sends data through Vercel and Resend. External services, including GitHub Pages, Vercel, Resend and Cal.com, apply their own security, cookie and privacy settings when you use them.

We do not use personal data collected through this website for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We may update this policy when the website, providers or legal requirements change; the date at the top identifies the current version.
