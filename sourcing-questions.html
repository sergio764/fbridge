What does FBridge do?

FBridge helps food and beverage companies in Europe find suppliers for their raw ingredients. You tell us the ingredient, specifications and quantities, and we contact suitable suppliers and collect quotes for you to compare.

Which ingredients can you source?

Our ingredient categories include cocoa powder, cacao nibs, green coffee, black tea, matcha and dates. We also take requests for other raw ingredients used in food and beverage production.

See the ingredients.

Where do you look for suppliers?

We look for producers across Latin America, Africa and Asia. The origins we consider depend on the ingredient and your requirements.

Can you find an alternative to my current supplier?

Yes. We can look for alternatives and collect quotes based on your specifications and quantities, so you can compare them with your current terms. You decide which suppliers you want to speak with.

How does the success fee work?

There is no upfront fee. FBridge earns a success fee if you decide to move forward with a supplier we introduce. The fee and scope of the work are agreed before the sourcing engagement begins.

What do you need to start a search?

Send us the ingredient, its specifications, the estimated quantity and when you need it. You can submit an ingredient request on our website or book a 15-minute call to discuss it.

Send an ingredient request or book a call.

Who is behind FBridge?

FBridge is a service operated by WEBRIDGE SOCIEDAD LIMITADA., based in Valencia, Spain. It grew out of weBridge's work with food and beverage companies, where finding ingredient suppliers was a recurring need.
